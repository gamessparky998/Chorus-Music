Chorus Music delivers a seamless, premium listening experience by leveraging YouTube Music's vast library — without the ads. It adds powerful extras including offline downloads, real-time synchronized lyrics, live video canvases, dual UI design systems, and environment-aware music recognition.

Features:
* Ad-Free — Stream without any interruptions.
* Dual UI System — Switch seamlessly between dynamic Material 3 Expressive and Apple Cupertino-inspired Fluid UI with real-time liquid glass shaders and player artwork morph transitions.
* Seamless Playback — Switch effortlessly between audio-only and video modes.
* Background Playback — Listen while using other apps or with the screen off.
* Offline Mode — Download tracks, albums, and playlists via a dedicated download manager.
* Multi-Source Synced Lyrics — Unmatched coverage powered by Musixmatch, lrc.red, BiniLyrics, YouLyPlus, BetterLyrics, Unison, PaxSenix, SimpMusic, LRCLIB, KuGou, Megalobiz, and Genius.
* Rich Word & Syllable Synchronization — Choose from glowing karaoke, word-by-word highlight animations, and real-time lyric translations.
* Live Canvases — Immersive animated album canvases powered by Apple Music, Tidal, and artist video providers.
* Chorus Find — Identify songs playing around you using advanced audio recognition.
* Smart Recommendations — Personalized suggestions based on your listening history.
* Chorus Brain — An intelligent, on-device engine that analyzes your listening momentum and auto-injects perfectly aligned tracks into your queue.
